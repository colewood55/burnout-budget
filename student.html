How It Works

Each round, the facilitator rolls the die to determine how many cards are drawn. Apply each card fully before moving to the next.

Fixed Costs

These minimums must be maintained after they are introduced.

  • Job/School Responsibilities: minimum 1 token
  • Bills & Essentials: minimum 2 tokens
  • Sleep & Rest: minimum 1 token

Gaining Tokens

If the card specifies a subcategory, place tokens there. Otherwise, place them in any subcategory you choose.

Losing Tokens

  • Take from another subcategory in the same domain first.
  • If the whole domain is empty, pull from any other domain.
  • Always maintain fixed cost minimums.

Conditional Cards

Check your current token count. If the condition is true, apply the top effect. If false, apply the “Otherwise” effect.

No Free Reallocation

In Rounds 3–5, only move tokens when a card instructs you to or when restoring a fixed cost minimum after a card causes a loss below the floor.
